# Sales Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and regional sales patterns.

The analysis was performed using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and profit
- Identify top-performing categories
- Evaluate regional sales performance
- Discover high-value customers
- Identify best-selling products
- Examine the relationship between discount and profit

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Overview

- Total Revenue: $2.30 Million
- Total Profit: $286 Thousand
- Average Sales per Transaction: $229.86
- Unique Orders: 5,009

### Category Performance

- Technology generated the highest revenue.
- Technology also delivered the highest profit.
- Furniture produced the lowest profit margin.

### Regional Analysis

- West region achieved the highest sales.
- South region recorded the lowest sales.

### Customer Insights

- Top 10 customers contributed significantly to total revenue.
- Sean Miller was the highest revenue-generating customer.

### Product Insights

- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest revenue-generating product.

### Discount Analysis

- Discount and Profit showed a negative correlation (-0.22).
- Higher discounts generally reduced profitability.
