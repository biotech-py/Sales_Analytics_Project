# Retail Sales Analytics Project Report

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze retail sales data and identify key business insights related to revenue generation, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends.

---

## Dataset Overview

- Total Records: 9,994
- Total Features: 21
- Missing Values: None
- Dataset Type: Retail Sales Transactions

---

## Revenue Overview

| Metric | Value |
|----------|----------|
| Total Revenue | $2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Average Sales per Transaction | $229.86 |
| Unique Orders | 5,009 |

### Key Insight

The business generated over $2.29 million in revenue with approximately $286 thousand in profit, indicating healthy overall performance.

---

## Category Analysis

### Revenue by Category

1. Technology
2. Furniture
3. Office Supplies

### Profit by Category

1. Technology
2. Office Supplies
3. Furniture

### Key Insight

Technology was the strongest-performing category in both revenue and profit generation. Furniture generated high sales but comparatively low profit margins.

---

## Regional Analysis

### Revenue Ranking

### Key Insight

The West region contributed the highest revenue and represents the company's strongest market.

## State Performance

### Top Revenue-Generating States

## Customer Analysis

### Top Revenue-Generating Customer

Sean Miller

### Observation

The top 10 customers contributed a meaningful portion of total sales revenue, highlighting the importance of customer retention strategies.

---

## Product Analysis

### Best-Selling Product

Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier

### Observation

A small number of products generated a disproportionately large share of revenue, suggesting the presence of high-value products within the portfolio.

---

## Discount vs Profit Analysis

### Correlation

- Discount-Profit Correlation: -0.22

### Key Insight

The negative correlation indicates that increasing discounts generally reduces profitability. Discount strategies should therefore be carefully managed.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus marketing efforts on Technology products.
2. Investigate ways to improve Furniture profit margins.
3. Expand successful strategies from the West region to lower-performing regions.
4. Strengthen retention programs for high-value customers.
5. Optimize discount policies to protect profitability.
